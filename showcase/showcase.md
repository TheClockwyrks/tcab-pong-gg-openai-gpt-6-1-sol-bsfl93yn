# Carom

Two paddles. Two turning obstacles. One clean return is never quite enough.
Carom is a top-down duel where every rally becomes a small puzzle of angles.
Take on the computer or challenge someone beside you on the same keyboard.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Meet the ball squarely for a
straight return, catch it near the edge for a sharper angle, or move through the
hit to put a curve on its flight. Spin fades as the ball travels, leaving a pale
trail that shows exactly how your shot bent.

The gold bars never stay still. They sway and rotate, turning a safe lane into a
bank shot and a desperate return into an unexpected point. Read their faces,
find the gap, and make the other paddle chase your ricochet.

Each paddle hit adds speed until the rally is a quick exchange of reflexes and
intent. Reach eleven with a two-point lead to finish the match; at ten-all,
you still have to earn the last word.
